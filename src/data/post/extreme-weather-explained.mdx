Extreme weather events are increasing in frequency and intensity, according to multiple scientific agencies.

## What’s happening
- Heatwaves are breaking seasonal temperature records.
- Flood events are more severe due to infrastructure strain.
- Storm systems are forming earlier in the year.

## Why it’s happening
Warmer oceans and a rising global temperature shift atmospheric patterns, creating more volatile conditions.

## Why it matters
Extreme weather affects:
- food supply  
- insurance markets  
- disaster responses  
- housing and infrastructure
