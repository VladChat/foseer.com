The U.S. presidential cycle is entering a critical phase. Candidates are refining their messages, key states are drawing national attention, and early policy clashes are defining the debate.

## What’s happening
- Major candidates are expected to announce their campaigns within weeks.
- Early polling shows voters prioritizing the economy, immigration, and healthcare.
- Party leadership is signaling a more aggressive primary season than in recent cycles.

## Why it matters
The early months often determine donor momentum, media framing, and the issues that dominate national conversation.

## What to watch
- Fundraising totals and first-quarter reports  
- Shifts in swing-state messaging  
- Early debates and policy rollouts
