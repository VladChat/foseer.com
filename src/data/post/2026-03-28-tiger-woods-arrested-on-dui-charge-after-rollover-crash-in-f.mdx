An event account cited by the New York Times reports that Woods was involved in a rollover crash in Florida and was subsequently arrested and charged with DUI. ## The Charge: What a DUI Case Typically Involves

A DUI charge generally alleges that a driver operated a vehicle while impaired, often by alcohol, drugs, or a combination of substances. The exact legal definition and penalties vary by state, but in Florida such cases commonly involve several standard steps:

- **Initial stop or crash response:** Officers respond to a crash or traffic stop and assess the driver’s condition.
- **Field assessment:** Police may conduct field sobriety tests or use a breath test device, depending on circumstances and local procedures.
- **Arrest and booking:** If officers believe there is probable cause for impairment, they arrest the driver and formally book them on DUI charges.
- **Chemical testing:** Blood or breath tests may be used to measure blood alcohol concentration or detect other substances.

The available reporting confirms that Woods was arrested and charged with DUI after the rollover crash but does not yet specify what kind of impairment is alleged or what testing, if any, was conducted. Without that information, it is not possible to say from current public evidence whether prosecutors will argue alcohol, drugs, or another factor was involved.
