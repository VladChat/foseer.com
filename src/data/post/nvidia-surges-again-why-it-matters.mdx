Nvidia’s latest earnings report again exceeded expectations, driven by massive demand for AI infrastructure. The company’s GPUs remain the backbone of AI training worldwide.

## What’s happening
- Revenue from data center chips hit new highs.
- Cloud providers continue placing multi-billion-dollar GPU orders.
- Competitors are accelerating their own AI hardware efforts.

## Why it matters
Nvidia’s dominance affects:
- AI product timelines  
- Cloud pricing  
- Investment flows into chipmakers and startups  

## What’s next
Analysts expect sustained demand through 2025, but geopolitical risks and supply constraints could shift the momentum.
