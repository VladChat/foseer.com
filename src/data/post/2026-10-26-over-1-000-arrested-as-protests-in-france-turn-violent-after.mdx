More than 1,000 people were arrested across France after clashes between protesters and police on Saturday evening, according to an event report from CBS News. Authorities said the confrontations followed a day of largely peaceful demonstrations held in multiple locations around the country.

CBS News reported that police described the unrest as concentrated in the evening hours, when some gatherings turned confrontational and officers moved in to restore order. The outlet did not provide a full breakdown of arrest locations but attributed the nationwide arrest figure to French police statements.

## Protests Largely Peaceful Before Evening Clashes

According to CBS News, demonstrations throughout France during the day remained mostly peaceful. Participants gathered to voice their grievances in organized marches and rallies before tensions escalated later in the evening.

Police told CBS News that clashes began after dark, when some protesters confronted officers and isolated pockets of unrest developed. The report did not specify the exact cities or neighborhoods where the most serious incidents occurred, but it stated that the arrests were spread across multiple protest sites.

Law enforcement officials, as cited by CBS News, said they intervened when demonstrations shifted from peaceful assembly to confrontations that they deemed a threat to public order. Details on the specific charges faced by those arrested were not included in the initial reporting.

## Scale of Arrests and Police Response

The arrest figure—more than 1,000 people nationwide—was reported by CBS News based on information from French police. The outlet characterized the number as a response to evening clashes rather than to the daytime protests as a whole.

CBS News reported that officers moved in to disperse crowds where confrontations intensified. The report did not provide a full account of police tactics or whether non-lethal crowd-control measures such as tear gas or water cannons were used.

Authorities did not immediately release comprehensive information on injuries among protesters or police, and CBS News did not cite any confirmed casualty totals in its coverage. The extent of property damage, if any, was also not detailed in the available reporting.
