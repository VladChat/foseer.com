A Louisiana man has been charged in the deaths of a woman on a hiking trail north of New Orleans and a co-worker killed two days later, according to law enforcement accounts reported by the Guardian.

Authorities say the man, identified as Jared Schurr, was arrested in Mississippi after the two otherwise unrelated killings, which occurred days apart in Louisiana.

## What police say happened

According to details reported by the Guardian, investigators allege that Schurr recently strangled a woman he did not know along a hiking trail north of New Orleans. The woman was identified as Lizeht Rodriguez.

Police told the outlet that Rodriguez was attacked and killed on the trail and that she had no prior connection to Schurr. The Guardian report describes the killing as a random assault based on the information authorities have released so far.

Two days later, Schurr is accused of killing a co-worker, identified as Woodrow Scott, with a crossbow. Law enforcement officials cited in the Guardian account say the second killing occurred in a separate incident and was not connected to the hiking trail attack other than the suspect allegedly involved.

After Scott’s death, authorities began searching for Schurr. He was later arrested in Mississippi, according to the Guardian’s reporting, which attributes the information to local law enforcement statements.

## Victims identified in separate attacks

The Guardian report names the woman killed on the trail as Lizeht Rodriguez and the co-worker killed two days later as Woodrow Scott. Police described the two deaths as otherwise unrelated, aside from the allegation that the same suspect was responsible.

Rodriguez was reportedly strangled during the attack on the hiking trail. Scott was killed with a crossbow, according to law enforcement accounts cited by the Guardian.

Officials have not publicly detailed any motive for either killing in the information reported so far. The Guardian article does not cite any law enforcement claim that the two victims knew each other.

## Arrest and charges

Schurr was taken into custody in Mississippi following the two killings, according to the Guardian’s account of law enforcement statements. The report states that he has been charged in connection with both deaths.

Authorities in Louisiana are handling the homicide investigations. The Guardian report does not specify the exact charges or list all counts, but describes them as stemming from the killings of Rodriguez and Scott.

Law enforcement agencies involved in the case, as described in the Guardian article, have not released a detailed public timeline of Schurr’s movements between the two killings and his arrest. The report focuses on the allegation that the hiking trail killing and the crossbow killing occurred two days apart.

## Why the case is drawing attention

The Guardian report notes that the first killing involved a woman attacked on a public hiking trail by a man she did not know, and that the second killing involved a co-worker allegedly shot with a crossbow. The unusual combination of a random trail killing and a crossbow homicide, along with the short time span between them, has made the case stand out in local reporting.

Because the investigation is ongoing and publicly available information is limited to what law enforcement has released and what the Guardian has reported, key details remain unclear. Those include any suspected motive, the full list of charges, and how investigators connected Schurr to each killing before his arrest in Mississippi.
