Detroit Lions cornerback Terrion Arnold has been arrested in Florida on felony charges including kidnapping and armed robbery in connection with what authorities describe as a targeted plot, according to reporting by the Guardian published on Wednesday.

Arnold, 23, is accused by Florida authorities of acting as a “primary conspirator” in the incident. A Florida attorney cited in the Guardian’s account said the potential sentence attached to the most serious counts could reach up to life in prison if he is convicted. The Lions have declined to comment on Arnold’s legal situation, the outlet reported.

Details of the alleged kidnapping and robbery come from charging information and statements described in the Guardian’s reporting. Florida officials have charged Arnold in connection with what they characterize as a planned, targeted crime, rather than a random encounter.

Those allegations form the basis for the kidnapping and armed robbery counts.

## Charges and possible penalties

The Guardian’s reporting indicates Arnold faces charges that include kidnapping and armed robbery under Florida law. In Florida, kidnapping is typically defined as forcibly, secretly, or by threat confining, abducting, or imprisoning another person against their will, often with an added purpose such as committing a felony or inflicting harm. Armed robbery generally refers to taking property from another person using a firearm or other deadly weapon, or threatening to do so.

A Florida attorney quoted in the Guardian article said that, based on the charges as described, Arnold could face a maximum sentence of up to life in prison if convicted on the most serious counts. That potential penalty reflects the severity with which Florida law treats kidnapping and armed robbery, particularly when prosecutors allege planning or conspiracy.

The Guardian report does not specify whether prosecutors have filed additional related counts, such as conspiracy or weapons charges, beyond the headline allegations of kidnapping and armed robbery.
