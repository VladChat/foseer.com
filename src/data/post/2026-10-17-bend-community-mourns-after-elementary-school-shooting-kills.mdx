A shooting at Silver Rail Elementary School in Bend, Oregon, on Thursday left three children and one teacher dead, according to reporting by the Guardian, throwing the city into grief and uncertainty as families waited hours for word on their loved ones.

Parents were directed to a nearby church, where they gathered for updates and reunification with students who had been evacuated from the campus. Among them was Joe Camarlinghi, who told the Guardian he waited there for three hours to learn whether his daughter was safe.

## What is known about the shooting

The Guardian reported that the shooting occurred on Thursday afternoon at Silver Rail Elementary, a public school in Bend. Local authorities had not released a full timeline of events or detailed information about the suspect at the time of that report.

According to the Guardian’s account, families first learned of the incident through an alert about a shooting at the school. Sirens could be heard in the area as law enforcement and emergency responders moved toward the campus.

Parents arriving at the school were directed away from the scene to a church used as a reunification point, where they were asked to wait while officials accounted for students and staff. It was from there, the Guardian reported, that many families learned whether their children had survived.

The Guardian’s report stated that three children and one teacher were killed. The identities of the victims had not been officially released in that account, and authorities had not publicly confirmed all details of the shooting.

## Hours of waiting and fear for families

The Guardian described scenes of parents and guardians waiting inside the church for hours, many of them trying to reach their children by phone while police secured the school and organized transportation for students.

Camarlinghi told the Guardian that after receiving the alert, he drove straight to Silver Rail Elementary, where he was redirected to the church along with other parents. He said the waiting was agonizing as families tried to piece together what was happening from scattered information and official briefings.

The Guardian reported that some parents were quickly reunited with their children, while others waited far longer, not knowing whether their child was among the victims. Community members told the outlet that the deaths of three children and a teacher had shaken the town and left many residents focused on the young lives lost.

## Community shock and early response

Residents interviewed by the Guardian described a sense of disbelief that a deadly shooting had taken place at an elementary school in Bend. People gathering near the church and school spoke about the children who had been killed and the teacher who died, emphasizing how difficult it was to process the loss.

The Guardian reported that the phrase “you can’t stop thinking about the kids” captured what many in the community were feeling in the immediate aftermath. Parents, school staff, and local residents spoke of the difficulty of explaining the violence to surviving children and of comforting families who had lost loved ones.

Local institutions, including the church that served as the reunification site, were drawn into the emergency response, according to the Guardian’s account. The church provided space for families, while law enforcement coordinated information and reunifications there.

The deaths of three children and a teacher at Silver Rail Elementary, as reported by the Guardian, have immediate and profound consequences for the families involved and for the Bend community. The shooting has forced parents, students, and educators to confront the loss of young lives and a classroom teacher in a setting that is usually associated with safety and routine.
