An 18-year-old gunman opened fire with a shotgun inside a vocational high school in Turkey, wounding at least 16 people before being killed, according to local authorities cited by CBS News. The victims include 10 students, four teachers, a canteen employee and a police officer, the regional governor said.

Officials described the shooting as a sudden, random attack inside the school. Independent corroboration of some details remains limited, and figures could change as authorities update their reports.

## What Officials Say Happened

Citing statements from the local governor, CBS News reported that the attacker entered a vocational high school armed with a shotgun and began firing indiscriminately. The shooting took place during the school day, when classrooms and common areas were occupied.

Authorities said at least 16 people were wounded:

- 10 students
- 4 teachers
- 1 canteen employee
- 1 police officer

The attacker was identified as an 18-year-old. Officials said he fired at random rather than targeting specific individuals, based on their initial assessment shared with reporters.

The governor said the gunman was killed, though the available reporting did not specify whether he died by police fire, self-inflicted injury, or other causes. That detail had not been independently clarified in the early accounts.

## Condition of the Victims

Local officials quoted in the CBS News report said the wounded were taken to nearby hospitals for treatment. The breakdown of victims suggests that both classroom and support staff, along with students and on-site security, were caught in the line of fire.

Authorities did not immediately release detailed information on the medical condition of each victim. Early reports focused on the total number of wounded and the categories of people affected, rather than specific injuries or prognoses.

Because this information comes from initial official briefings, it may be updated as hospitals and emergency services provide more precise assessments.

## How Authorities Responded

The presence of a wounded police officer among the victims indicates that law enforcement was on the scene during or shortly after the attack, though the exact timeline has not been fully described in the initial reporting.

According to the governor’s account relayed by CBS News, security forces moved in to stop the attacker inside the school. The gunman was later confirmed dead. Officials did not immediately provide a detailed sequence of events, such as how long the shooting lasted, how many shots were fired, or the exact locations within the school where the violence occurred.

The use of a shotgun, as reported by the governor, suggests a weapon capable of causing multiple injuries in confined spaces like classrooms or hallways. Authorities have not publicly detailed how the attacker obtained the firearm.

## What Is Known and What Is Still Unclear

Based on the CBS News account, several core facts have been established by official statements:

- The shooting occurred at a vocational high school in Turkey.
- The attacker was 18 years old.
- He was armed with a shotgun.
- At least 16 people were wounded: 10 students, four teachers, a canteen worker and a police officer.
- The gunman is dead.

Other points remain less clear in the early reporting:

- The attacker’s motive has not been publicly disclosed.
- The precise timeline of the attack and police response has not been fully outlined.
- The current medical status of the wounded has not been detailed victim by victim.

CBS News noted that independent corroboration of some elements is still limited at this stage. As with many fast-moving incidents, early numbers and descriptions can shift as more agencies report in and as investigators review security footage and witness statements.

## Why This Incident Matters

A shooting inside a high school, even when the attacker is quickly neutralized, has immediate consequences for students, staff and families. The number of wounded in this case—spanning children, educators, support staff and a police officer—highlights how many people can be affected in a matter of minutes when a firearm is brought into a school setting.

Officials in Turkey are likely to face questions about how the attacker gained access to the campus with a shotgun, what security measures were in place, and how emergency protocols functioned once the shooting began. Those discussions had not yet been fully reported in the initial CBS News account.

For now, the confirmed facts center on the scale of the injuries, the age of the attacker, and the rapid acknowledgment by local authorities that the gunman fired randomly inside a school before being killed. Further reporting from Turkish officials, local media and international outlets will be necessary to fill in the remaining gaps about motive, security, and any policy responses that may follow.

Readers should watch for updates from Turkish authorities and additional verified news reports, which are likely to refine the casualty figures, clarify the sequence of events, and provide more detail on how the attack unfolded inside the school.
