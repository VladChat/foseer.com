Currently, U.S. and Israeli forces have been targeting Iranian military assets for the second consecutive week, marking an escalation in hostilities that many anticipated but had not fully realized as a "hot" war. In retaliation, Iranian forces and their proxies have engaged across the Middle East, highlighting the regional implications of this conflict ([Source](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/dispatches/twenty-questions-and-expert-answers-about-the-iran-war)).
