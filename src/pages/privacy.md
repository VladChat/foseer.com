_Last updated_: March 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Foseer may collect, how we use it, and what choices you have when you visit [https://foseer.com](https://foseer.com).

## Scope

This policy applies to the public website, article pages, and direct communications you send to us by email or phone. Foseer does not currently offer user accounts, paid memberships, on-site comments, or a public newsletter sign-up.

## Information We May Collect

### Information you provide directly

- Your name, email address, phone number, and any other information you choose to include when you contact us.
- News tips, corrections, media inquiries, business inquiries, or other messages you send to us.

### Technical information collected automatically

- Basic server and hosting logs, such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, requested URL, and access time.
- Limited browser storage used to remember your theme preference on this site.

## How We Use Information

- To operate, secure, and improve the website.
- To respond to questions, tips, corrections, and support requests.
- To detect abuse, malicious traffic, fraud, or technical issues.
- To keep internal records reasonably necessary for editorial, legal, and operational purposes.

## Cookies, Analytics, and Similar Technologies

Foseer currently uses limited local browser storage to remember interface preferences such as dark or light theme selection. We do not currently run a configured third-party analytics ID or advertising network on the live site.

If we later enable analytics, advertising, newsletter tools, or other technologies that materially change our data practices, we will update this Privacy Policy and related notices.

## How We Share Information

We may share information only in the following limited situations:

- With hosting, infrastructure, email, security, or technical service providers that help us operate the site.
- When required by law, court order, subpoena, or a valid government request.
- To protect the rights, safety, property, security, or operations of Foseer, our users, or the public.
- As part of a business transfer, sale, merger, or reorganization involving the site or its assets.

We do not sell or rent personal information that you provide to us through direct communications.

## Retention

We keep communications, technical logs, and related records only for as long as reasonably necessary for security, editorial operations, recordkeeping, dispute resolution, and legal compliance.

## Your Choices

- You can choose not to contact us or not to provide optional information in your messages.
- You can disable or clear browser storage through your browser settings, although some display preferences may reset.
- You can request access, correction, or deletion of personal information you have directly provided to us, subject to legal and operational limits.

## Children's Privacy

Foseer is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the public site.

## External Links

Our articles and pages may link to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party services.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version becomes effective when posted on this page.
